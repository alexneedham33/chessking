Balance scale & calibration

The centre is fixed at 50/50. A difference of 1 evaluation pawn from the starting position becomes about 58/42; 2 pawns about 66/34; 3 pawns about 73/27; 5 pawns about 84/16. These values are a chosen display scale, not probabilities.
